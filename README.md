# UAV Wing Modal Analysis

## 1. Project Overview
This project focuses on the analytical study of vibration characteristics of a UAV wing.

## 2. Objectives
- Understand the vibration behaviour of a UAV wing.
- Study bending and torsional vibration modes.
- Calculate natural frequencies using analytical methods.
- Understand the effects of stiffness, mass and wing length on natural frequency.

## 3. Methodology
The UAV wing will be modelled as a cantilever beam. Analytical calculations and engineering diagrams will be used to study its free vibration characteristics.

## 4. Tools and Methods
- Engineering calculations
- Beam vibration theory
- Engineering diagrams

## 5. Project Scope
The study will initially focus on the bending vibration modes of a simplified UAV wing model.
