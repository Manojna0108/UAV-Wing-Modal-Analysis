# Week 1 – Project Planning

## 1. Project Title
UAV Wing Modal Analysis Using Analytical Methods

## 2. Introduction
Aeroelasticity is the study of the interaction between aerodynamic forces, structural elasticity and inertia.

A UAV wing experiences different loads and vibrations during flight. Studying its vibration characteristics helps us understand its structural behaviour.

Modal analysis is used to determine the natural frequencies and mode shapes of a structure.

## 3. Project Objectives
1. To understand the basic concepts of aeroelasticity.
2. To study the bending vibration behaviour of a UAV wing.
3. To understand natural frequency and mode shapes.
4. To estimate natural frequencies using analytical methods.
5. To study the influence of material stiffness, mass and wing dimensions.

## 4. Methodology
The UAV wing will be simplified as a cantilever beam for the initial analytical study.

The wing root will be considered fixed, while the wing tip will be considered free.

Beam vibration theory will be used to estimate the natural frequencies and understand the bending mode shapes.

## 5. Initial Assumptions
- The wing is modelled as a uniform beam.
- The wing material is assumed to be homogeneous and linearly elastic for the simplified model.
- The wing root is fixed.
- The wing tip is free.
- Initial analysis considers free vibration.

## 6. Scope of the Project
The initial study focuses on the bending vibration modes of a simplified UAV wing. Torsional vibration and coupled aeroelastic effects are outside the scope of this initial model.

## 7. Expected Outcome
The project aims to understand the vibration characteristics of a simplified UAV wing and estimate its natural frequencies using analytical calculations.

## 8. Project Status
Week 1 – Planning and Aeroelasticity Basics.
